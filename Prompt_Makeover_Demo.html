The 5 ingredients of a great prompt

Most excellent prompts mix these together:

1. Clear task"Explain...", "Write...", "Make a plan..."
2. Audience / context"for a 7th grader", "for my best friend"
3. Format & length"3 bullet points", "150 words", "a table"
4. Role"Act as a friendly tutor / coach / author"
5. Tone & extras"keep it funny", "avoid jargon", "add an example"

    Teacher talking points

    • Same AI, different result: The model didn't get smarter between steps - the prompt got clearer. Better questions get better answers.
    • Specific beats long: A short, specific prompt beats a long, vague one. Audience + format do the heavy lifting.
    • Role sets the voice: "Act as a coach / author / tutor" steers tone and word choice.
    • Iterate: Real prompting is a back-and-forth - "make it shorter", "add an example", "simpler words".
    • Still verify: Even an excellent prompt can produce a confident wrong answer (a hallucination). Always check facts.
    Try with the class: Start at Step 1, ask students to predict the AI's answer, then press "Improve it" and compare. Then have them score their own prompts in Demo 2 and aim for "Excellent".
