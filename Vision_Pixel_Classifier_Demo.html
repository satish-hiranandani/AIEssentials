Peek under the hood (how it decides)
Class demo: Draw a clean O - very confident. Now draw a Q (an O with a tail) and watch O and Q fight for first place. Try P vs R, or E vs F. Ask: why does AI mix up look-alike letters?

How this maps to real Vision AI (teacher notes)

  • Images are numbers: Turn on "Show numbers" - every pixel is a 1 (dark) or 0 (white). That grid is all the AI ever sees.
  • It normalizes first: The AI centers and resizes your drawing before comparing, so it doesn't matter where or how big you draw. Real vision systems do this too.
  • Pattern matching: It scores your pixel pattern against all 26 letters and ranks them - the top 3 are shown with confidence.
  • Look-alikes are hard: B/D/P/R, C/G, E/F, M/N/W, O/Q, U/V often compete. This is genuinely why real models need thousands of varied examples per letter, not one template.
  • Great lead-in: Use the confusions to motivate Teachable Machine - a model that learns from many examples beats a single fixed template.
