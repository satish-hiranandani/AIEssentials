How this maps to real Vision AI (teacher notes)

  • Images are numbers: Turn on "Show numbers" - every pixel is a 1 (dark) or 0 (white). The AI never sees a picture, only this grid of numbers.
  • Pattern matching: It compares your dark-pixel pattern to a stored pattern for each shape and scores the overlap - the closest match wins.
  • Confidence, not certainty: The bars are percentages. A messy drawing lowers confidence - the AI is guessing, not "knowing".
  • Real models learn the patterns: Here the shapes are pre-set. Real vision AI learns them from thousands of labeled images (like in Teachable Machine), but the idea is identical: pixels in, pattern match, confident guess out.
  • It can be fooled: Draw a sloppy shape and the AI gets confused - a great lead-in to bias and "adversarial" mistakes.
